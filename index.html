Our pitch

SmartRooms in one minute.

The problem

No one can watch every screen all day.

Bullying goes unnoticed

Many incidents happen when no teacher is looking, and victims often don't report them.

Exams are hard to supervise

One invigilator can't see every desk, so copying and malpractice slip through.

Cameras record, but nobody reviews

Footage is checked only after a complaint, when it's already too late to step in.

How it works

Three steps, no new cameras.

  1. Connect your cameras

    SmartRooms works with your school's existing CCTV setup. It's software only.

  2. AI reviews the footage

    Computer vision looks for risky behaviour in classrooms, corridors and exam halls.

  3. Your staff get an alert

    The right person sees what was flagged, with the key video frames, on a simple dashboard.

What it detects
